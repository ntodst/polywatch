THE RESEARCH METHOD

Consistency needs time.

The long-term screen requires every condition below. An unmet condition stays visible, so a large lifetime profit never substitutes for evidence.

  1. Fresh account verification

    Overall realized plus unrealized position PnL is nonnegative. Stale or incomplete accounts remain pending; persistently disqualified sources stay in the audit archive.

  2. 90 days observed locally

    At least 60 covered daily comparisons, positive net observed PnL, at least 30 active days, and at least 60% of those days positive. Missing days are not filled or counted as zero.

  3. Historical consistency

    Complete fetched closed history covering at least 30 markets; positive all-time realized and monthly marked PnL; 10 active daily samples, 60% positive; covered open PnL and positive realized plus open PnL. Analysis must be refreshed within one hour.

  4. Prospective copy evidence

    At least 20 fully priced simulated closes spanning 30 days, with positive total net PnL after estimated fees. Skips, open positions, and losing or excluded sources remain recorded.

Drawdown is the peak-to-trough dollar decline across daily account PnL samples. It may miss intraday losses. Returns are not expressed as ROI because invested capital is not known.

Ranks cover discovered and manually shortlisted wallets. They are not a ranking of every Polymarket account. Simulations currently follow qualifying BUY alerts and exit on source exit, disqualification, or a 24-hour horizon; they do not replicate every source strategy.
